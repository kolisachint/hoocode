◭Same philosophy, new cockpit

The five rules still hold: attention is the budget, three layers, everything is a block, two views (peek and radar), and status circles that read without colour. What changes is the cockpit: a one-row HUD, numbered steps on a trajectory, and a comms frame whose colour is the state. The metaphor is in the chrome; the log stays literal. Tool names, paths and numbers are never renamed.

◐The cockpit

Four rows of chrome

The HUD (1 row): mode, folder, ship, fuel, credits. Comms (3 rows, heavy frame): its colour is the state. Faint means READY, cyan IN FLIGHT, amber CLEARANCE, red COMMS LOST, green LANDED. Its bottom border shows peek or radar and the tokens.

Two views

Peek is the trajectory: every step numbered, with up to three lines of what it saw, and one landing line at the end. Radar is the flight log: one row per mission with steps, hits, time and credits. ctrl+o switches between them.

In ratatui

HUD, commsParagraph · Block::bordered().border_type(Thick) with top and bottom titles
Trajectory, logParagraph of styled Lines
ConsolesList or Table inside a thick Block
Fuel, progressLineGauge, or block Spans
ToastsClear + Block over a Rect
Wordmark, QR▀ ▄ ▟ ▙ spans with Color::Rgb

Every glyph is one terminal cell. Nothing needs Canvas, images or extra crates.
