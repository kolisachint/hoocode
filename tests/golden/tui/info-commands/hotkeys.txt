«» «bold;fg=#f0c674»### What the chord tells you

«» «fg=#5cc8bb»Alt«»+letter «bold»sets a value«» and nothing takes the screen — you keep typing.
«» Seven of those are dials, and «fg=#5cc8bb»Shift«» always steps one back: «bold»a«»gent mode,
«» «bold»m«»odel, «bold»t«»hinking, tool «bold»o«»utput, task «bold»l«»ist, session «bold»c«»olor, and
«» chrome («bold»z«», the one letter that names nothing — its stop is the shape of the
«» screen in front of you).

«» «fg=#5cc8bb»Ctrl«»+letter «bold»acts on what is drawn right now«» and shares its letter with the
«» «fg=#5cc8bb»Alt«» key for the same subject. «fg=#5cc8bb»Alt+O«» sets how much tool output there
«» ever is, «fg=#5cc8bb»Ctrl+O«» jumps to all of it and back; «fg=#5cc8bb»Alt+T
«» sets how much thinking there ever is, «fg=#5cc8bb»Ctrl+T«» shows or hides what you
«» have. Two subjects, two letters, four keys.

«» The thinking level also answers to «fg=#5cc8bb»Shift+Tab«» — it is the one dial with no
«» slash command, so that is the way to it on a terminal that does not send «fg=#5cc8bb»Alt«».

«» Inside a picker every «fg=#5cc8bb»Ctrl«» key still edits the query, so the picker's own verbs
«» are on «fg=#5cc8bb»Alt«» and its hint line names them.
